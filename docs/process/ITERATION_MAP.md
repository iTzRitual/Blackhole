private advisory prompts. The coding trajectories and recorded evaluation files
are the evidence for implementation and results.

## Reading the map

The V1 rows describe the scientific development track and its later
generalization check. The Product V2 rows describe post-freeze development
acceptance and human dogfood. Neither should be read as a single monotonic
scoreboard. A future frozen Product V2 head-to-head comparison is a separate
evaluation with its own cases, contract, metrics, and limitations.
